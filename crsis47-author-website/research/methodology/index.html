CRSIS-47 / Methodology

Evidence unfolds. Assessments evolve.

A public overview of the ongoing research approach to progressive detection and evidence-based cybersecurity decision-making.

T+0 through T+47

A sequence.
A changing assessment.

This overview describes the research framework. It does not report experimental outcomes or establish independent validation.

01

Release the initial evidence

T+0 represents the release of the first scenario evidence package to the evaluated investigator or agent. Subsequent checkpoints introduce additional evidence according to the research protocol.

02

Introduce evidence progressively

The framework examines a defined sequence of evaluation checkpoints from T+0 through T+47. Assessments are studied as additional evidence is introduced, including supporting and exculpatory evidence.

03

Examine timing and classification

The research considers time to confident detection (TTCD), threshold-qualified detection, malicious, benign, and uncertain classifications, false-positive threshold crossings, missed detections, and final classification accuracy.

04

Keep human oversight central

Human oversight, explainability, and responsible AI-assisted decision-making are part of the research focus. The aim is a reproducible framework for examining timing, reliability, and uncertainty.
