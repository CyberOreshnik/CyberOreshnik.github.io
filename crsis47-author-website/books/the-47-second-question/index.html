T+47 / 01 — Know

Book One · Know

The 47-Second Question

By Constantin Jean-Pierre

What happens when the truth arrives one piece at a time—and a decision cannot wait?

Synopsis

By the time a victim discovers the damage, the event already has a history. Somewhere in that history may be a signal that mattered. But when did it become enough to justify a warning?

The 47-Second Question follows Constantin Jean-Pierre from a personal experience of digital intrusion into the intellectual struggle behind CRSIS-47. The urgent wish to have known sooner meets a harder demand: show what the evidence actually supported at the time. A strange login is not a verdict. An alert is not the whole story. And a fast answer can create its own harm when certainty outruns context.

Written with the human stakes still in view, the book follows questions of detection timing, false positives, uncertainty, and explanation. The developing research is part of that inquiry, not a promise that every attack can be stopped on a universal clock.

As KNOW, the first stage of T+47, it leaves readers with the question that drives the series forward: when a decision cannot wait for the complete picture, what does responsible knowing require?
