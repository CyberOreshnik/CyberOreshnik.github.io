Constantin Jean-Pierre is an author and cybersecurity researcher pursuing doctoral studies at Capitol Technology University.

Constantin Jean-Pierre connects his ongoing doctoral work to CRSIS-47’s investigation of progressive evidence disclosure, detection timing, and uncertainty. His research asks how AI-assisted cybersecurity assessments can change responsibly as evidence develops, with explainable reasoning, human oversight, and accountability central to consequential decisions.

T+47 and CRSIS-47 are distinct literary and academic bodies of work, connected by their interest in evidence, time, intelligent systems, and consequential decisions.
