01

Evidence before inference.

Separate what the record shows from what an analyst or model infers. Preserve uncertainty when evidence does not justify a conclusion.

02

Time is part of the evaluation.

Examine how assessments change as additional evidence becomes available across defined evaluation checkpoints.

03

Human judgment remains central.

Keep human oversight, explainability, and responsible AI-assisted decision-making at the center of security assessments.
